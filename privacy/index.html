Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version. You can use FindFilm.ai without an account, and then your watchlist and ratings stay in your own browser. If you choose to sign in, we store them so they follow you across devices. We don't sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

FindFilm.ai runs this website. For anything in this policy, write to support@findfilm.ai.

If you don't have an account

These are kept in your browser's storage on your device. They aren't sent to us, and clearing your browser's site data removes them:

  • your watchlist and the dates you changed it
  • your ratings and reviews, and the name you give with them
  • your taste profile, built from the films you open
  • your country and minimum-rating filters, and display settings such as language

If you used our old address, ratingkino.com, your browser sends these to us so they can be moved to findfilm.ai. We delete our copy as soon as it has been moved, or after ten minutes at the latest.

Signing in

An account is optional. It is only needed to save and sync across devices. You sign in with Google; we have no passwords. From Google we receive an account identifier, your email address and your name. We use them to recognise you and to contact you about your account, and we don't use your Google account for anything else.

When you sign in we store the following on our servers, and merge in what was saved in your browser:

  • your account identifier, email address and name
  • your watchlist, ratings, reviews, taste profile and filter settings, with the dates they changed
  • a sign-in session, kept in a cookie that is essential for staying signed in

What you send when you use a feature

  • Search. What you type is processed by AI models to find films.
  • Photo search. A photo you choose is sent to an AI model to read its mood. We don't keep the photo.
  • Music search. A link you paste is looked up to read the song's mood.
  • Voice search. This uses your browser's speech recognition, which may send your audio to your browser's provider.
  • Movie Pitch. Votes are stored with a random identifier from your browser. Comments and the name you give are public to anyone with the link.
  • Location. We use your approximate country, from your connection, to choose a language and show what's streaming near you.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your data from our database straight away; it can remain in backups for up to 30 days, after which it is gone.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and its data from your account menu, or by writing to us.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including objecting to how we use your data and complaining to your data protection authority.

Children

FindFilm.ai isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell signed-in users about any important change.
